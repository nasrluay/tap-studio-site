Privacy Policy

Applies to Chomp Loop, Door Jam and Trust Issues: Wall Kick. Last updated 9 November 2026.

The short version

Our games don't collect personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no ad trackers and no analytics SDKs. Your progress stays on your device.

What stays on your device

  • Game progress, coins, boosters, skins and settings are saved on your phone only. Deleting the app deletes them.
  • In Chomp Loop, if you choose "Chomp your photo", the photo you pick is turned into a level on your phone. It is never uploaded or shared, and we can't see it.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We never see your name, email or payment details. Apple tells the app only which item was bought so it can be delivered. Apple's privacy policy applies to the payment.

Data we collect

None. We don't collect, sell or share personal information, and we don't track you across apps or websites.

Children

The games are suitable for all ages and do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes

If this ever changes (for example, if we add crash reporting), we'll update this page and the App Store privacy details before the update ships.
