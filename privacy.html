Privacy Policy

Applies to Chomp Loop, Bit Jam and Trust Issues: Wall Kick. Last updated 11 November 2026.

The short version

There are no accounts and no analytics SDKs, and your progress stays on your device. The games have no pop-up ads. If you choose to watch a short video for a reward, that video comes from Google's AdMob, which collects some device data to show and measure the ad (details below).

What stays on your device

  • Game progress, coins, boosters, skins and settings are saved on your phone only. Deleting the app deletes them.
  • In Chomp Loop, if you choose "Chomp your photo", the photo you pick is turned into a level on your phone. It is never uploaded or shared, and we can't see it.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We never see your name, email or payment details. Apple tells the app only which item was bought so it can be delivered. Apple's privacy policy applies to the payment.

Reward videos (Google AdMob)

Some screens offer a reward, such as free coins or a free continue, for watching a video. Videos only play when you tap one; skipping them never blocks the game.

The videos are provided by Google AdMob. To choose, show and measure an ad, and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect:

  • Device identifiers: the advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking when the app asks) and an app-specific identifier.
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address.
  • Ad interactions, such as which ads were shown and tapped.
  • Diagnostics, such as crash and performance data from the ad SDK.

We don't receive this data ourselves; Google processes it under the Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Your choices:

  • Tracking: the app asks once whether it may use your advertising identifier. You can change this any time in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. If you say no, you still get reward videos, just less personalised ones.
  • Europe, the UK and Switzerland: you're shown Google's consent form before any ad, and "Ad choices" in the game's settings lets you change your answer.

Data we collect

We don't collect, sell or share personal information ourselves. The only third party that receives device data is Google AdMob, as described above, and only for the reward videos.

Children

The games are not directed at children under 13 and don't knowingly collect personal information from them. Reward videos are limited to content rated for general audiences (Trust Issues: parental guidance).

Changes

If this changes, we'll update this page and the App Store privacy details before the update ships.
