Support

Lost a purchase?

Open the game, tap ⚙️ Settings, then Restore purchases. Skins and permanent perks come back on any device using the same Apple ID.

Refunds

Apple handles refunds: reportaproblem.apple.com.

Stuck on a level?

  • Chomp Loop: send out chompers whose color is on the edge of the picture first, and keep a seat free on the bench.
  • Bit Jam: the clock starts on your first move. Clear the blocks in front of doors first.
  • Trust Issues: every level is beatable. Promise. The 👁️ Spoiler shows every trap.
